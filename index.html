MedicationAlert Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

MedicationAlert is a personal medication reminder app developed by Christo Booysen. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

Data storage

All medication information you enter — names, dosages, schedules, quantities on hand, and notes — is stored locally on your device only, in a private database. This data is never uploaded to any server. MedicationAlert does not have user accounts, logins, or cloud sync of any kind.

Network use

MedicationAlert makes a network request in one situation: when you scan a medication's barcode, the app sends that barcode to a shared, anonymous medication-lookup service to try to find the medication's name, strength, and form automatically. No personal information, health history, or device identifier is sent with this request — only the barcode, and, if you choose to contribute a new medication to the shared catalog, its general product details (name, dosage, form, manufacturer). This lookup service is shared across a small family of apps and has no way to know who is using it.

If you don't have a network connection, or choose not to scan, every field can be entered manually and the app works fully offline.

Permissions

  • Camera — used only to scan barcodes; no photos or video are stored or transmitted.
  • Notifications, exact alarms, and "ignore battery optimizations" — used to reliably deliver medication reminders at the time you scheduled.
  • Run at device startup — used to reschedule your reminders after your phone restarts.

Data sharing

MedicationAlert does not sell, rent, or share your medication data with advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third party. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs in this app.

Data deletion

Since everything is stored locally, uninstalling the app permanently deletes all your medication data. You can also delete individual medications or dose history at any time from within the app.
